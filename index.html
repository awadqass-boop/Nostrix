Questions

Before you book.

Where do you work?

We are based in Ras Al Khaimah and shoot across the UAE. Travel outside Ras Al Khaimah is one fixed fee per booking: Umm Al Quwain AED 75, Ajman AED 125, Sharjah AED 150, Dubai AED 200, Abu Dhabi AED 300 and Al Ain AED 350.

How do I book?

Send a brief or message us on WhatsApp. We confirm the scope, deliverables, timeline and price in a written quote. A 50% deposit books the date. No online payment is needed on this website.

When do I get my files?

On the delivery date agreed in your quote. 24-hour delivery is available for an extra 30%.

How many revisions are included?

One revision round, unless your package states otherwise.

Can I get the RAW files?

Yes. RAW photo files are an extra AED 250. RAW video footage starts at an extra AED 500.

Do you offer monthly content?

Yes. Creator Monthly is AED 1,490 per month and Business Content is AED 2,490 per month. Both have a 3-month commitment and are prepaid monthly.

What is not included in the price?

Travel outside Ras Al Khaimah, venue permits, studio hire, rentals, ad spend and other third-party costs. Each one is listed separately in your quote.
